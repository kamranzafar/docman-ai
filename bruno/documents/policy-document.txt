ACME CORPORATION
REMOTE WORK POLICY

Policy Number: HR-POL-014
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Last Reviewed: 2026-05-15
Owner: Human Resources Department

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes guidelines for employees who work remotely on a full-time or
hybrid basis, ensuring consistent expectations around availability, security, and
performance across the organization.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees approved for remote work
arrangements by their department manager and Human Resources.

3. ELIGIBILITY
Employees must have completed their introductory 90-day period and maintain a
satisfactory performance record to be eligible for remote work arrangements.

4. EQUIPMENT AND SECURITY
- Employees must use company-issued laptops with up-to-date endpoint protection software.
- All company data must be accessed through the corporate VPN.
- Employees must lock their workstation when unattended.

5. WORKING HOURS AND AVAILABILITY
Remote employees are expected to be available during core business hours
(10:00 AM - 3:00 PM local time) and to respond to messages within one business day.

6. PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
Remote work arrangements are subject to ongoing review and may be revoked at the
discretion of management if performance or availability standards are not met.

7. POLICY REVIEW
This policy will be reviewed annually by Human Resources and may be updated to reflect
changes in business needs or applicable regulations.

Approved by: VP of Human Resources
