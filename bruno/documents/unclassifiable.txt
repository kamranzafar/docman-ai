My Favorite Sourdough Bread Recipe

Ingredients:
- 500g bread flour
- 350g water
- 100g active sourdough starter
- 10g salt

Instructions:
1. Mix flour and water, let rest for 30 minutes (autolyse).
2. Add starter and salt, mix thoroughly.
3. Perform 4 sets of stretch and folds over 2 hours.
4. Bulk ferment for 4-6 hours at room temperature.
5. Shape the dough and place in a banneton.
6. Cold proof in the fridge overnight.
7. Bake in a preheated dutch oven at 250C for 20 minutes covered, then 20 minutes uncovered.

Enjoy your fresh sourdough bread with butter or olive oil!
